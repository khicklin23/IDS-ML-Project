What changed, and why

Accuracy means the share of sessions guessed correctly. Ordinary tests cover familiar attacks; slowed-down tests vary their pace. Timing tests contain the same measurements in a different order, making the sequence itself the signal.

Swipe sideways to compare every test.

Why move beyond Beta 2? More examples improved its coverage, but individual-connection guesses lose the order of events. Beta 3 tested a small memory across connections. Sona combined that memory with broader attack training. The goal was to recognize more kinds of behavior, not to run faster. Speed has not been measured.
